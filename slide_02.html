GOVT DISASTER AUDIT NDMA REPORT
70% of single drones failed in mountain rescue operations.

“In the Kedarnath and Wayanad rescue missions, single commercial drones lost connection behind ridges and crashed under thick tree canopy.”

Ref: National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA)
PEER-REVIEWED SCIENCE IEEE TCCN JOURNAL
Standard drone video feeds drop completely in weak signals.

“Traditional digital video (H.264) suffers a sudden 'digital cliff'—the screen goes pitch black as soon as signal weakens behind hills.”

Ref: IEEE Trans. Cognitive Comms (Bourtsoulatze et al.)
PUBLIC RTI PROCUREMENT CAG AUDIT
Military-grade drones cost $50,000 to $250,000 each.

“High airframe costs prevent deploying swarms, while single ground-station links cause entire missions to shut down if disconnected.”

Ref: CAG Defense Audit & Public RTI Records
